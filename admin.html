Scream & Win: how hard is it to win?

The numbers behind it

The round is one quick shout: it ends as soon as the guest has shouted and gone quiet, or when the scream window runs out. To win, a guest needs the overall score AND the minimum loudness AND the minimum clarity. Clarity only passes for a clear "Wendy's" with the S; only their first attempt at the word counts. Loudness is the loudest 0.6 seconds (dBFS) from the kiosk mic. Calibrate on site: a normal talking voice at 1 m should read near the floor, a full scream near the ceiling.
