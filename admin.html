Scream & Win scoring

To win, a guest needs the overall score AND the minimum loudness AND the minimum clarity. Clarity only passes for a clear "Wendy's" with the S; only their first attempt at the word counts. Loudness is the loudest sustained half-second (dBFS) from the kiosk mic. Calibrate on site: a normal talking voice at 1 m should read near the floor, a full scream near the ceiling.
