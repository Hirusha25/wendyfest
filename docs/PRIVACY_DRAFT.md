## 2. What we collect and why

**When you register**
- Your name, mobile number and preferred language. We need these to give you a Wendy Fest code, send it to you by SMS and show your name on the games.
- Your email address and date of birth, only if you choose to give them. [Confirm what these are used for, or remove the fields.]
- Whether you agreed to receive marketing messages.

**When you play Scream & Win**
- Your voice is recorded for up to 10 seconds, only to measure how loud you were and to check which word you said.
- We keep your score, the word that was heard, and technical details about the microphone. We do not keep the recording.
- If you win, we keep the coupon code given to you.

**When you play Roast Me**
- One photo of you, taken at the kiosk only after you tap to agree on the consent screen.
- The roast written about your photo, in English, Sinhala and Tamil, and the card colour you chose.

**On your phone**
- One cookie that remembers your phone has registered, kept for up to one year, so you can reopen your pass. A setting that remembers your language. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

**Messages**
- A record of the SMS messages we send you (your code, your results, your roast link).

## 3. Companies that help us

We share only what each one needs to do its job:

| Company | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TextWare (SMS provider, Sri Lanka) | Your mobile number and the text of the SMS | To deliver your code and results |
| Anthropic (AI provider, USA) | Your Roast Me photo | To write the roast. The AI is instructed to comment only on things you chose, such as clothes or accessories |
| ElevenLabs (voice provider, USA/EU) | Your Scream & Win recording; the text of your roast | To recognise the word you shouted; to read the roast aloud in Wendy's voice |
| [Hosting provider] | All the data above, stored on our server | To run the app |

The cut-out of your photo for the roast card is made inside the browser of the kiosk or your own phone. The photo is not sent to anyone else for that step.

Some of these companies are outside Sri Lanka, so your photo, recording and roast text are processed abroad. [Legal to confirm the wording required for cross-border transfers under the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.]
